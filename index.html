How Shamba Fast Kenya Works

For Farmers:
1. Fill Farmer Form above - What you have + Price + Location
2. You get a Sale ID like SK-124 - save it
3. We try to find a buyer
4. Buyer contacts via Shamba Fast Kenya
5. Agree price + delivery / pickup BEFORE sale
6. Report SALE DONE / NO SALE with your Sale ID

For Buyers (Mama Mboga, Hotels, Schools, Homes):
1. Fill Buyer Form
2. You get a Sale ID
3. We try to find a farmer who has it
4. Agree price + pickup / delivery
5. Pay farmer directly. Pay AFTER you see the produce.

Trust & Safety

- Farmer's phone number is NEVER shown on the website. You buy via Shamba Fast Kenya.
- We do NOT show "Verified" unless we have checked it.
- Phone Checked = We called the number and spoke to the farmer.
- ⭐ Top Farmer = 3+ sales reported DONE, no complaints.
- New Listing = Just joined, phone check pending.
- 2x NO SHOW reports = Farmer / Buyer is removed.
- Always agree price, quantity, quality and pickup/delivery BEFORE sale.
- Do not pay in advance unless you agree it yourself. Check produce before paying.
